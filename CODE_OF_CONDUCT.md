# Code of Conduct

AutoTuneNet adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept technical criticism about code, results and claims, not about people.
- Report benchmark results honestly, including results that do not favour this project.
- Help newcomers; a question is never a waste of anyone's time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@mitishraina](https://github.com/mitishraina), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. If the matter concerns a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Every report will be reviewed and handled confidentially, and reporters will not face retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant. Consequences follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
